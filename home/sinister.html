Once You See Him, Nothing Will Be The Same

A true-crime writer moves his family into a new home and discovers a collection of disturbing films that reveal a terrifying mystery. As he investigates the recordings, he uncovers a supernatural presence that puts his family in danger.

Directed By: Scott Derrickson

Cast: Ethan Hawke, Juliet Rylance, Fred Thompson, James Ransone and Michael Hall D'Addario
