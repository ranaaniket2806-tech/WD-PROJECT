Parthiban, a mild-mannered café owner and animal rescuer in a quiet town in Himachal Pradesh, becomes a local hero after thwarting a deadly robbery. His newfound fame inadvertently attracts the attention of a ruthless drug cartel led by Antony and Harold Das, who suspect he is their estranged family member, Leo Das, a fearsome former gangster.

Directed By: Lokesh Kanagaraj

Cast: Vijay, Trisha, Sanjay Dutt, Arjun Sarja, Gautham Vasudev Menon

Genre: Action Thriller

Year of Release: 2023
