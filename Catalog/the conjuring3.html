The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It

The demonic case that shocked America

Parormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren take on one of the most sensational cases of their careers after a cop stumbles upon a dazed and bloodied young man walking down the road. Accused of murder, the suspect claims demonic possession as his defense, forcing the Warrens into a supernatural inquiry unlike anything they have ever seen before.

Directed By: Michael Chaves

Cast: Patrick Wilson, Vera Farmiga, Ruairi O'Connor, Sarah Catherine Hook, Julian Hilliard

Genre: Supernatural Horror | Mystery | Thriller

Year of Release: 2021
