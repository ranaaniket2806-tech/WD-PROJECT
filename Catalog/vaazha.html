Vaazha

Biopic of a Billion Boys

Five childhood friends labeled as "useless" by their parents and society struggle under the crushing weight of expectations. As they face academic failures, societal judgment, and family pressure into adulthood, their unbreakable friendship becomes their ultimate support system in finding their own purpose in life.

Directed By: Anand Menen

Written By: Vipin Das

Cast: Joemon Jyothir, Siju Sunny, Hashir H, Saaf Bros, Amith Mohan Rajeswari, Jagadish

Genre: Coming-of-Age | Comedy | Drama

Year of Release: 2024
