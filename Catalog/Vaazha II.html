VAAZHA II

Biopic of a Billion Bros

Vaazha II: Biopic of a Billion Bros is a Malayalam comedy-drama that follows a group of young friends as they navigate friendship, family expectations, relationships, and the challenges of growing up. The film blends humor and emotion while celebrating the bond between friends.

Directed By: Anand Menen

Cast: Pranav Raj, Hashiree, Ziya Vincent, Althaf Salim

Genre: Comedy | Drama

Year of Release: 2025
