THE NUN

Pray for Forgiveness

When a young nun at a secluded abbey in Romania takes her own life, the Vatican sends a priest and a novitiate to investigate. Together, they uncover a dark and terrifying secret involving a demonic force that threatens their lives and their faith.

Directed By: Corin Hardy

Cast: Taissa Farmiga, Demián Bichir, Jonas Bloquet, Bonnie Aarons

Genre: Horror | Mystery | Thriller

Year of Release: 2018
