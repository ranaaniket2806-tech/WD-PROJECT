Vaazha

Biopic of a Billion Boys

Five carefree friends navigate the intense pressure of parental and societal expectations. Labeled as "underachievers" as they transition into adulthood, they embark on a journey of friendship, self-acceptance, and finding their own paths despite being constantly judged.

Directed By: Anand Menen

Written By: Vipin Das

Cast: Siju Sunny, Amith Mohan Rajeshwari, Joemon Jyothir, Anuraj O.B., Anshid Anu, Jagadish, Basil Joseph

Genre: Comedy | Drama | Coming-of-Age

Year of Release: 2024
