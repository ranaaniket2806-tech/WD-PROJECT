Toxic

A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups

Set against the atmospheric backdrop of post-independence Goa under Portuguese rule, Raya, a ruthless and ambitious gangster, navigates a dangerous underworld governed by crime, ambition, and shifting loyalties. As power struggles escalate, he is forced to balance dark alliances, personal moral dilemmas, and the pursuit of redemption.

Written & Directed By: Geetu Mohandas

Cast: Yash, Kiara Advani, Nayanthara, Huma Qureshi, Tara Sutaria, Rukmini Vasanth

Genre: Action | Crime | Psychological Thriller

Year of Release: 2026
