Kaithi

LCU - Lokesh Cinematic Universe

Dilli, an ex-convict recently released from prison, tries to meet his daughter for the first time in ten years. However, his plans are interrupted when an injured inspector enlists his help to drive a truck full of poisoned police officers to safety while evading a relentless drug syndicate.

Written & Directed By: Lokesh Kanagaraj

Cast: Karthi, Narain, Arjun Das, George Maryan, Dheena

Genre: Action | Suspense | Mystery | Drama | Thriller

Year of Release: 2019
