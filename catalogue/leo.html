Parthiban, a mild-mannered cafe owner and animal rescue volunteer in Himachal Pradesh, becomes a local hero after thwarting a gang of robbers. However, his life takes a dangerous turn when powerful gangsters mistake him for Leo Das, a ruthless dangerous criminal from their past.
