The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It

The Conjuring Universe

Paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren uncover a chilling story of terror, murder, and unknown evil that shocks even them. Starting with a fight for the soul of a young boy, the case takes them into uncharted territory as a murder suspect claims demonic possession as his defense in U.S. court history.

Directed By: Michael Chaves

Written By: David Leslie Johnson-McGoldrick & James Wan

Cast: Patrick Wilson, Vera Farmiga, Ruairi O'Connor, Sarah Catherine Hook, Julian Hilliard

Genre: Horror | Mystery | Thriller

Year of Release: 2021
