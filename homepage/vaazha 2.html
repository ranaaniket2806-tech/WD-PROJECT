Vaazha II

Biopic of a Billion Bros

Four friends—Hashir, Alan, Ajin, and Vinayak—are considered losers and troublemakers by their parents and society. Facing immense social pressure, they embark on an emotional journey of self-discovery, learning to embrace adulthood and find success on their own terms in this coming-of-age bromance.

Directed By: Savin S.A

Written By: Vipin Das

Cast: Hashir H, Alan Bin Siraj, Ajin Joy, Vinayak V, Aju Varghese, Vijay Babu

Genre: Comedy | Drama

Year of Release: 2026
