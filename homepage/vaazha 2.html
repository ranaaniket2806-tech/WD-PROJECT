Vaazha II

Biopic of a Billion Bros

Four close friends—Hashir, Alan, Ajin, and Vinayak—navigate the chaotic highs of youth and the harsh realities of growing up. Labeled as troublemakers, they face immense social and parental pressure, embarking on a turbulent journey of self-discovery, responsibility, and testing whether their friendship can survive the weight of the real world.

Directed By: Savin SA

Written By: Vipin Das

Cast: Hashir, Alan Bin Siraj, Ajin Joy, Vinayak V, Alphonse Puthren, Vijay Babu, Aju Varghese, Sudheesh

Genre: Comedy | Drama | Coming-of-Age

Year of Release: 2026
