The Conjuring

Based on the True Case Files of Ed and Lorraine Warren

In 1971, paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren are called to assist the Perron family, who are experiencing terrifying, supernatural events in their newly acquired Rhode Island farmhouse. As they dig into the home's dark history, they uncover a malevolent demonic presence threatening the family's lives.

Written & Directed By: Directed by James Wan (Written by Chad Hayes & Carey W. Hayes)

Cast: Vera Farmiga, Patrick Wilson, Lili Taylor, Ron Livingston, Joey King, Mackenzie Foy

Genre: Supernatural horror | haunted house

Year of Release: 2013
