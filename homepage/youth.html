Youth

A Story of Adolescence and True Love

Praveen, a carefree 15-year-old schoolboy entering adolescence, becomes determined to find true love before his school life comes to an end. Navigating classroom mischief, teenage heartbreaks, and changing relationships with his friends and family, Praveen undergoes a journey of emotional maturity that shapes his outlook on life.

Written & Directed By: Ken Karunas

Cast: Ken Karunas, Anishma Anilkumar, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Devadarshini, Priyanshi Yadav

Genre: Coming-of-Age |Romantic Comedy | Drama
