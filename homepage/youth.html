Youth

A Story of Love and Friendship

Shiva, a carefree young man, moves to Chennai to escape an arranged marriage and instantly falls in love with a woman named Sandhya. However, complications and comedic situations arise when his cousin, the very girl he was supposed to marry, also arrives in the city and genuinely falls for him, creating a complex love triangle.

Directed By: Vincent Selva

Cast: Vijay, Shaheen Khan, Sindhu Menon, Vivek, Yugendran

Genre: Romantic Comedy | Drama

Year of Release: 2002
