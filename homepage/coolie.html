Coolie

STANDALONE ACTION THRILLER

Deva, a former coolie union leader with a dark past, steps back into action to uncover the mystery behind his close friend's death. As he digs deeper into a high-stakes gold smuggling operation, he confronts powerful crime syndicates and rivalries rooted decades in the past.

Written & Directed By: Lokesh Kanagaraj

Cast: Rajinikanth, Nagarjuna, Soubin Shahir, Upendra, Shruti Haasan, Sathyaraj

Genre: Action | Crime | Thriller

Year of Release: 2025
