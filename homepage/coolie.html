Coolie

Thalaivar 171

A high-octane action thriller following Devaraj, a former coolie union leader who runs a boarding house in Chennai. When his friend dies under mysterious circumstances, Deva is pulled into a dangerous web of gold smuggling, undercover cops, and an international crime syndicate led by the ruthless Simon Xavier.

Written & Directed By: Lokesh Kanagaraj

Cast: Rajinikanth, Nagarjuna, Soubin Shahir, Shruti Haasan, Sathyaraj, Upendra

Genre: Action Thriller

Year of Release: 2025
