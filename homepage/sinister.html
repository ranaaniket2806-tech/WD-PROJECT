Sinister

Once you see him, nothing can save you.

A controversial true-crime writer finds a box of Super 8 home movies in his new home, revealing that the murder he is currently researching is the work of a gruesome supernatural serial killer. As he delves deeper into the footage, he unwittingly puts his own family in grave danger.

Written & Directed By: Scott Derrickson

Cast: Ethan Hawke, Juliet Rylance, Fred Dalton Thompson, James Ransone

Genre: Horror | Mystery | Thriller

Year of Release: 2012
