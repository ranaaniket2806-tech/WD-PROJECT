MAIN LADEGA

Fight for What Matters

Main Ladega is a Hindi sports drama about a young man who discovers boxing and decides to fight against the challenges in his life. His journey is filled with struggle, determination, family challenges, and the courage to never give up.

Directed By: Gaurav Rana

Cast: Akash Pramanik, Jitendra Kumar, and others

Genre: Drama | Sports | Action

Year of Release: 2024
