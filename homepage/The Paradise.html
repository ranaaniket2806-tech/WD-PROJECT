THE PARADISE

A Story of Power and Survival

The Paradise is an upcoming action drama set in Hyderabad. The story follows a powerful and dangerous world filled with crime, conflict, power struggles, and survival. As the characters face difficult challenges, their lives become connected through a struggle for power and justice.

Written & Directed By: Srikanth Odela

Cast: Nani, K. P. V. Sunil, Ranjin Raj, and others

Genre: Action | Drama | Thriller

Year of Release: 2026
